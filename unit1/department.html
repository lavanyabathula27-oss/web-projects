Commerce

The Department of Commerce was established in the year 1974. The courses offered in B.Com stream are Computer Applications, Business Analytics and Taxation. BBA course was introduced last year. The Permanent Lecturers in the Department are four in number. Our primary objective is to enable every student to cope up with the latest developments in contemporary, national and global level through effective transaction of the curricular and co-curricular aspects.


Bio-Technology

The Department of Biotechnology has been established during the year 2019-20. The department primarily studies about molecular biology related to different organisms with special reference to human beings. Physiological processes like DNA Replication, Protein Synthesis (Gene Expression) etc. Study projects related bio-waste and hospital waste are some potential areas of student research carried in our department.



Mathematics

Mathematics department offers various Mathematics courses to all the Under Graduate science streams. Our objective is to make our students more competent, motivated engineers and scientists with a strong mathematical background. The Department of Mathematics has 3 well experienced faculty members. All faculty members pursued their qualifications from reputed Universities of India and have a track record of good research.
